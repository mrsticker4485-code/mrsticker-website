Signs You Need Brake Service

Squeaking or squealing

A high-pitched sound when you stop often means worn brake pads.

Grinding

Metal-on-metal grinding can damage rotors. Get it checked right away.

Soft or spongy pedal

The pedal sinks further than it used to before the car stops.

Vibration or pulling

Shaking in the wheel or pedal, or the car pulls to one side when braking.

Brake warning light

Any brake light on the dashboard is worth a same-day look.
